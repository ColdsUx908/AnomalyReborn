[h1]异象 / Anomalies[/h1]

[h2]中文[/h2]

异象（Anomalies）是一个以战斗体验为核心的泰拉瑞亚内容模组。
模组引入了异象模式，为部分原版 Boss 重新设计攻击方式与阶段转换，赋予熟悉的战斗全新面貌。
此外，模组还加入了新物品，调整了部分原版武器，并提供可配置的玩家移动与建造速度提升。

使用裂天金冕开启或关闭异象模式，在大师难度下迎接新的挑战。
对于渴望更高难度的玩家，传奇难度下还可开启异象超凡模式，在严苛的挑战中突破极限。

[h3]模组的主要内容包括：[/h3]
[list]
[*] 部分原版 Boss 的战斗重制，包含新的攻击方式、弹幕与战斗机制
[*] 异象模式，异象超凡模式
[*] 自定义 Boss 血条、主菜单等视觉效果
[*] 一系列新物品与开发者专属物品
[*] 对部分原版武器的调整
[*] 可配置的玩家基础属性提升
[*] 与灾厄模组的可选联动
[/list]

[b]本模组需要安装大洋彼岸（Transoceanic）框架作为前置。[/b]
推荐搭配音乐模组（AnomaliesMusic）。
灾厄模组为可选搭配。

[h2]English[/h2]

Anomalies is a combat-focused content mod for Terraria.
It introduces Anomaly mode and redesigns attacks and phase transitions for selected vanilla bosses, bringing a fresh experience to familiar battles.
The mod also adds new items, adjusts selected vanilla weapons, and offers configurable improvements to player movement and building speed.

Use the Heavenrend Crown to enable or disable Anomaly mode and face new challenges in Master difficulty.
For players seeking an even greater challenge, enable Anomaly Ultramundane in Legendary difficulty and push your limits through demanding battles.

[h3]The mod's main features include:[/h3]
[list]
[*] Reworked encounters for selected vanilla bosses, featuring new attacks, projectiles, and combat mechanics
[*] Anomaly mode and Anomaly Ultramundane mode
[*] Custom boss bars, a custom main menu, and other visual effects
[*] A variety of new items and special developer items
[*] Adjustments to selected vanilla weapons
[*] Configurable boosts to player base stats
[*] Optional integration with the Calamity Mod
[/list]

[b]This mod requires the Transoceanic framework.[/b]
The companion music mod, AnomaliesMusic, is recommended.
The Calamity Mod is optional.
